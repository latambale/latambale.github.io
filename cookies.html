Cookie Policy

Prototype notice. BuildKhata keeps tracking to a minimum.

We do not use tracking cookies

BuildKhata does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other websites.

Local storage we do use

To make the app work, we store a few items in your browser's local storage (not traditional cookies):

  • Sign-in token: a short-lived signed token so you stay logged in.
  • Selected project: remembers which project you were viewing.
  • App cache: a service worker caches the app's files so it loads fast and works offline.

These stay on your device and are not shared. Clearing your browser's site data removes them and signs you out.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a couple of script libraries from public CDNs to render charts and PDFs. These may receive your IP address as part of serving those files, per their own policies.

Control

You can clear local storage at any time from your browser settings. The app will simply ask you to sign in again.
