Privacy Policy

Prototype notice — last updated on load. This describes how the BuildKhata prototype handles data.

Your data lives in your Google Sheet

BuildKhata stores your projects, transactions, vendors and invoices in a Google Sheet that you own. We do not run a separate database of your business data. The app reads and writes that Sheet through a Google Apps Script backend that runs under your own Google account.

Secrets & keys

API keys (OpenAI, email) are stored only in your Apps Script project settings on Google's servers. They are never sent to the browser or the mobile app, and they are never stored in the app's code.

Voice & AI

When you use voice entry, your device converts speech to text, and that text (not audio) is sent to the OpenAI API through the backend to extract the transaction details. Only the sentence you spoke is sent; it is used to produce the structured entry and is subject to OpenAI's data policies.

Email

If you enable email reports, summaries are sent via Resend to the address you configure. We don't use your email for anything else.

What we store in your browser

A short-lived sign-in token and your chosen backend URL are stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Clearing site data removes them.

Contact

This is a prototype built for a specific user. For questions, contact the project owner.
